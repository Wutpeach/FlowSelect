## Evidence consistency resolution

The original source/production contradiction was a timestamp/capture bug, not
a second renderer. `playAvatarAnimation` begins a clip in a 500ms
`neutral -> first-step` transition, so the old source labels at `0ms`,
`2550ms`, and `2800ms` did not represent the claimed curious holds/midpoint.
The older Lab helper also assumed an 18s quiet deadline and used a one-shot
page random override that unrelated startup work could consume.

Both helpers now identify their pipeline and sample the correct action-relative
points: `800ms` first hold, `3050ms` second-step midpoint, and `3700ms` second
hold. The direct-core helper uses Node 22 type stripping to import the exact
production Compact definition, so it cannot drift into a separately copied
source definition. The browser helper sets `Math.random` to `0.45`, giving the
known `24,300ms` quiet deadline, and records each **actual** capture time in
the sheet and matrix rather than labelling a target time as an observed frame.

`research/evidence/curious-evidence-frame-matrix.json` retains compact
per-frame facts only: pipeline, action-relative timestamp/phase, expression,
the two fixed nodes, and direct-core or production-DOM back-front path hashes,
bounds, and centroids. It retains no raw SVG paths. The direct reference has:

- `800ms`: `expression-00`, head `[7.3, 27.8, -16.1]`, back hashes
  `c154b697c11a` and `36cd0603e6a5`.
- `3050ms`: a real smooth `00 -> 15` transition with head
  `[3.8095703125, 31.5537109375, -13.5021484375]`.
- `3700ms`: upstream `expression-15`, head
  `[0.319140625, 35.307421875, -10.904296875]`, back hashes
  `81703e7712bc` and `8082a87c0031`.

The authoritative Lab captures landed at `25,120ms`, `27,357ms`, and
`28,020ms` after click: action-relative `820ms`, `3057ms`, and `3720ms`.
The hold hashes match the direct-core holds. The DOM cannot expose the private
playback frame, so the midpoint retains its browser-observed DOM statistics
and identifies its exact direct-core target separately at `3050ms`; it makes
no false hash-equality claim across the 7ms sample difference. The focused
runtime regression supplies the direct same-frame projection proof.

Focused runtime coverage now pins the distinct upstream endpoint heads and
proves the Compact runtime's head, eyes, and two ear paths equal
`renderAvatarFrame` for the same playback frame. The fixed body nodes and
perspective are unchanged, so the differing curious ear paths are attributable
to the explicitly recorded head pose, not an ear-side path transform.
