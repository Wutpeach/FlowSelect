# Unified Thermal Energy to Panel Boundary Response

## Goal

Produce a repository-grounded architecture plan for one unified Thermal causality:

```text
Ameow 2D Thermal Energy
→ interior Thermal material
→ Heat Haze / Refraction
→ response at the real rounded panel boundary
   → localized edge energy
   → subtle 14px exterior halo
```

The accepted Ameow 2D multi-temperature field must be the sole Thermal energy authority. Paper-derived mechanics may inform boundary treatment, contour/inner-outer transition, and subtle halo language, but must not own interior morphology.

## Preconditions

- Preserve the rejected Paper Internal Morphology and Boundary-Driven experiments as evidence.
- Restore production and tests to the accepted Thermal + Refraction checkpoint.
- Archive the rejected tasks and begin this task from a clean worktree except for this planning directory.

These preconditions were completed before this task was created. The accepted production baseline remains the Thermal + Refraction checkpoint; archive commits contain Trellis evidence only.

## Locked Architecture

- Accepted 2D multi-temperature interior material and palette.
- Accepted Heat Haze / Refraction identity.
- Native/output domain: 228×228.
- Visible and interactive panel: 200×200, radius 16, origin (14,14).
- Transparent gutter: 14px per side.
- Real rounded panel boundary.
- One `ExpandedPresentationSurface`, one canvas, one WebGL renderer/program, one draw, and existing renderer-local runtime authority.
- Existing Reduced Motion authority.
- No native window-bounds expansion.
- Accepted Thermal + Refraction checkpoint remains the stable fallback.

## Requirements

1. Identify the accepted Thermal scalar or energy representation best suited to be the single source of truth.
2. Define a boundary-local response that samples/modulates that same energy near the real rounded boundary without creating a second material or color authority.
3. Show how the same energy drives interior material, Refraction intensity, localized edge response, and exterior halo.
4. Define halo transport/falloff within the existing 14px gutter so output approaches zero naturally before the 228×228 outer edge.
5. Separate reusable Paper-spike infrastructure from Paper-specific morphology that must be removed.
6. Preserve normal panel/UI clipping, hit testing, drag/interaction semantics, singular CSS shadow ownership, and non-interactive FX canvas behavior.
7. Propose a minimal Browser Lab spike with independent evidence views that can falsify or confirm unified energy ownership.
8. Keep the plan bounded; do not introduce a generic field, scene, or choreography framework.
